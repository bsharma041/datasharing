Quick questions

Can I buy just one?

Yes. Pick single items and we pack them together. With single items, your items must total more than ₹299 before delivery; gift bags count toward it.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, anywhere in India, in 7–10 days from your order.

Is delivery free?

Items worth ₹999 or more ship free. Smaller orders pay ₹99, or ₹149 to the North East.

Add things to your bag on the website and pay online by UPI, card, netbanking or wallet, or send the same order on WhatsApp to 97306 24700 and we send you a payment link.
