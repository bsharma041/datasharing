Quick questions

Is there a minimum order?

Items you pick yourself must total more than ₹299. Ready-made gift bags have no minimum.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, anywhere in India, in 7–10 days from your order.

Is delivery free?

Items worth ₹999 or more ship free. Smaller orders pay ₹99, or ₹149 to the North East.

How do I order and pay?

Add things to your bag on the website and pay by UPI, or send the same order on WhatsApp to 97306 24700.

Will it look exactly like the photo?

Colours, characters or designs may vary with availability. The product type and value stay the same.
