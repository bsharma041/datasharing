    Good to know

    Questions

    Is there a minimum for custom bags?

    Yes. Items you pick for a custom bag must total more than ₹299. You pay only for the items you choose. Ready-made gift bags have no minimum.

    How long does dispatch take?

    We curate bags in small batches, so please allow 5–7 working days for dispatch.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes. Door-to-door delivery has an additional charge, which we confirm with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

    Will I get exactly the colour in the photo?

    Colours, characters or designs may vary with availability. The product type and value always stay the same.

    Can I buy single pencils?

    Yes. Our Unicorn and Racing Car 2B pencils come in a box of 12 for ₹109, or as single pencils at ₹15 each. Mention it in your WhatsApp message.

    Can I order many return gifts for a party?

    Yes. Add the return-gift bag you like, set the quantity, and send it to us. We'll confirm stock for larger orders.
