    Questions

    Is there a minimum for custom bags?

    Yes. Items you pick for a custom bag must total more than ₹299. You pay only for the items you choose. Ready-made gift bags have no minimum.

    How long does delivery take?

    7–10 days from your order, anywhere in India. Checkout shows the expected dates as soon as you enter your PIN code.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, anywhere in India. Orders with items worth ₹999 or more ship free. Smaller orders pay ₹99, or ₹149 to the North East (Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim and Tripura). Checkout shows your charge as soon as you enter your PIN code.

    Will I get exactly the colour in the photo?

    Colours, characters or designs may vary with availability. The product type and value always stay the same.

    Can I buy single pencils?

    Yes. Our Unicorn and Racing Car 2B pencils come in a box of 12 for ₹109, or as single pencils at ₹15 each. Mention it in your order note.

    How do I pay?

    By UPI, for your order total including delivery.

    Can I return or replace something?

    We don't accept returns or exchanges. If an item arrives damaged, or is wrong or missing, WhatsApp us on 97306 24700 within 24 hours of delivery with your order ID and an unedited unboxing video that starts from the sealed parcel. We replace only the affected items, not the whole bag or box. By default you get store credit for them, valid for 6 months. You can choose a replacement item instead, or a refund to your original payment method if you prefer. Approved refunds reach you within 5–7 working days.

    Can I cancel my order?

    Yes, any time before we dispatch it. WhatsApp us on 97306 24700 with your order ID. Once your order has been dispatched, it can't be cancelled. If you have already paid, we refund the full amount, including delivery, to your original payment method within 5–7 working days of the cancellation.

    Can I order many return gifts for a party?

    Yes. Add the return-gift bag you like, set the quantity, and send it to us. We'll confirm stock for larger orders.
