Five tips that save you stress

  1. Choose one theme. Racing cars, space or unicorns. A matching bag looks like a proper gift.
  2. Mix by who is coming. If both boys and girls are invited, choose a bag with a neutral theme like the Starter, or order two kinds.
  3. Think about ages. A pencil box, pens and a notebook suit school-age kids best. For a party of toddlers, a different kind of gift may suit better.
  4. Buy a few extra. Someone always turns up with a cousin.
  5. Order early. We deliver in 7 to 10 days, so order at least two weeks before the party.
