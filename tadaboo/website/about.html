About TadaBoo

TadaBoo is a small online shop for kids' stationery, craft kits and ready-packed gift bags. It is run by Upasana Negi from Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, and delivers anywhere in India.

What we sell

  • Gift bags: ready-packed return gifts, themed bags and birthday hampers.
  • Stationery: pens and highlighters, pencils and notebooks, sharpeners and erasers, craft kits, gift sets and squishies.
  • Build your own bag: pick items from the shop and we pack them together.

Every order comes with a thank-you card and a TadaBoo bookmark. Prices are in Indian rupees and include all taxes.

How ordering works

  1. Add items or a gift bag to your bag on this website and place the order with your name, phone and delivery address. You can also order on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay the order total, including delivery, by UPI from any UPI app. The website shows the exact amount and a QR code for your order.
  3. We confirm your order on WhatsApp or call, pack it, and ship it. Orders arrive in 7–10 days.

Delivery is free on orders of ₹999 or more. Below that it is ₹99, or ₹149 to the North East. Full details are in our delivery policy.

If something goes wrong

If an item arrives damaged, wrong or missing, WhatsApp us within 24 hours of delivery with your order ID and an unboxing video. We replace the affected items, or give store credit or a refund to your original payment method. You can cancel any time before dispatch for a full refund. See returns and replacements and cancellation and refunds.

Colours, characters or designs of some items may vary with availability. The product type and value always stay the same. Where this applies, the product page says so.
